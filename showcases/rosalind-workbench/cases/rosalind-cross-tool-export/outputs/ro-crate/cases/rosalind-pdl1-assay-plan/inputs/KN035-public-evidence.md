# Public evidence used for the PD-L1 assay plan

## Structural and biochemical reference

Lv et al., “Structural basis of a novel PD-L1 nanobody for immune checkpoint blockade,” *Cell Research* (2017), PMCID [PMC5341541](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5341541/), PDB [5JDS](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/5JDS).

The paper reports the PD-L1–KN035 complex and describes several assay types that inform this plan:

- phage clones were screened by ELISA before functional testing;
- KN035-Fc binding to PD-L1 was measured with a fortéBio Octet K2 instrument;
- the published kinetic protocol used Protein A sensors, 10 µg/mL KN035-Fc loading for 40 seconds, 60 seconds of equilibration, a twofold PD-L1 dilution series, 120 seconds of association, and up to 320 seconds of dissociation;
- competitive ELISA assessed inhibition of the PD-1/PD-L1 interaction;
- flow cytometry was used to examine target specificity.

Those observations are evidence that the assay modes are relevant to KN035. They are not measurements of the five designed candidates. The retained plan uses monomeric VHH candidates, so its BLI immobilization format and concentration series are explicitly proposed parameters that require a pilot experiment.
